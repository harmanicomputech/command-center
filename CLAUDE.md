# Command Center

Campaign platform for the Ebonyi State governorship election (6 Feb 2027). One Laravel 13 app with two faces: the **Command Center** (leadership, desktop first, sidebar layout) and the **Field Force** app (agents, phone first, offline-first PWA, bottom tab bar). MySQL in production, SQLite in-memory for tests. Deploys to DirectAdmin shared hosting with **no terminal and no per-minute cron**.

The full brief is `docs/HANDOFF.md`; progress and the owner's open decisions are in `docs/PROGRESS.md`. **Update both after every phase.**

## Commands

- `php artisan test` (or `scripts/test.sh` for a short summary): the whole suite, before every commit. **Before a release, also run it on MySQL/MariaDB** (the host's database): `DB_CONNECTION=mysql DB_HOST=127.0.0.1 DB_DATABASE=… DB_USERNAME=… DB_PASSWORD=… php artisan test` (CI's `test-mysql` job does this); SQLite misses MySQL's 64-character identifier limit, strict GROUP BY and non-transactional DDL
- `vendor/bin/pint`: format code (CI runs `pint --test`)
- `npm run build`: compile CSS/JS into `public/build/` (the host has no Node; the build ships in the zip)
- `scripts/build-shared-hosting.sh [--update|--ci]`: upload zips in `dist/`; CI uploads them as the `command-center-upload-packages` artifact
- `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/screenshots.mjs <dir> <login> <password> <paths…>`: screenshots at 360/768/1280 in light and dark, reports horizontal overflow and JS errors
- `node scripts/icons.mjs` (after adding a Lucide name), `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/make-icons.mjs` (PWA icons)
- `php artisan pu:import [csv]`, `php artisan app:tick`

## Layout

- **Design system:** `resources/css/tokens.css` (every colour, shadow and radius; a re-brand is this one file), `resources/css/app.css` (Tailwind v4 mapping + component classes: `.card`, `.btn-*`, `.input`, `.segmented`, `.badge-*`, `.table`, `.nav-item`, `.tabbar`), `resources/views/components/` (Blade components: `x-button`, `x-card`, `x-kpi`, `x-input`, `x-segmented`, `x-empty`, `x-table`, `x-modal`, `x-lga-map`, …), `resources/views/design/index.blade.php` (the `/design` living style guide: add every new component there). JS in `resources/js/` (Alpine: theme, ⌘K palette, toasts, `x-count`, PWA).
- **Layouts:** `components/layouts/app` (Command Center: sidebar ≥1024px, drawer on tablets, tab bar on phones), `layouts/field` (Field Force), `layouts/guest`, `layouts/base`. Navigation for sidebar, tab bar and palette comes from `App\Support\Navigation` (items appear once their route exists).
- **Roles and scope:** `App\Enums\UserRole` (admin, strategist, lga_leader, ward_coordinator, agent). `User::limitToArea($query, $wardColumn)`, `Ward::visibleTo()`, `Lga::visibleTo()`, `User::canSeeLga()/canSeeWard()`, and the `BelongsToWard` trait (`inAreaOf($user)`) enforce scope on the server; controllers `abort_unless(... , 403)`. Route middleware: `staff` (Command Center, not agents), `role:admin,...`. Agents sign in with phone + PIN, staff with email + password (`Console\AuthController`); `TrackActivity` signs out switched-off accounts.
- **Geography:** `lgas`, `wards`, `polling_units`, loaded by `App\Services\PollingUnitImporter` from `database/data/ebonyi_polling_units.csv` at first-admin setup (totals kept on wards/LGAs). `RegisterStatus` drives the register warning. `LgaMap` is the schematic tile map.
- **Field capture:** `public/outbox.js` (the IndexedDB outbox, `window.ccOutbox`, also `importScripts`ed by `sw.js` for Background Sync), `resources/js/outbox.js` (Alpine: `$store.outbox` and the sync pill, `registerForm`, `outboxList`), `POST /api/field/sync` → `App\Services\Field\FieldSync` → one `SyncHandler` per item type (`RegisterVoter`; add tasks/issues/surveys to `FieldSync::HANDLERS`). Handlers must be idempotent on the item UUID and throw `ValidationException` for items that can never be accepted. `App\Models\Voter` (encrypted `phone` + `phone_hash`, `counted()` excludes invalid and unresolved duplicates, `visibleTo()`), `config/canvass.php` (form options, consent text + version), `Console\VoterController` (verification, duplicates, export), `Console\TeamController` + `App\Services\Invitations` + `InviteController` (invite links; `sessions_revoked_at` + `auth_at` in the session sign a lost phone out), `AgentStats`.
- **Structure and CRM:** `App\Services\Structure` (engagement from `lastOutput()`: registrations, event attendance, and tasks via `extraOutputs()`; `wardHealth()` red rules from `config/structure.php`), `Console\PeopleController`, `StructureController`, `InfluencerController` (`Influencer`, encrypted `contact_phone`), `EventController` (`Event::visibleTo()`, `event_user` attendees, month calendar). Ward profile: `AreaController::ward`.
- **Tasks, issues, gamification:** `Task`/`TaskReport` (`Task::for($user)`: theirs or their ward's), `Issue` (`ACCEPTED` statuses earn points), `Console\TaskController`, `IssueController` (map, trend, briefing pack), `LeaderboardController`; field pages in `FieldController`; sync handlers `ReportTask`, `ReportIssue`. Photos: `App\Services\PhotoStore` (private disk, re-encoded JPEG + thumb, idempotent by UUID), `PhotoController` (`/api/field/photos` answers 409 until the owning record has synced; `/photos/{photo}` checks the viewer can see the owner), `x-photo-field` + `resources/js/photos.js` (shrinks on the phone), outbox `addPhoto()`. Morph map in `AppServiceProvider`. `App\Services\Points` computes points from records (never stored), `Badges`, `Review` (private pattern flags).
- **Intelligence and the dashboard:** `App\Services\Intelligence` (zones, certainty, priority, trend per ward/LGA; weights and thresholds in `SettingsRegistry` 'intelligence'; reach/tag presets as `intel.reach.<slug>`/`intel.tag.<slug>` settings; add survey shares in phase 6 as a third source), `PastResultImporter` + `ResultsController`, `PresetController`, `Segments` + `SegmentController` (counts only), `DailyBrief` (dashboard + `/brief`, `notify()` at 07:00 from `BackgroundRunner`), `MapLayers` (layers for the LGA tiles and the ward map), `WardMap` (uploaded GeoJSON → SVG paths on the private disk; `x-ward-map`). Web Push: `PushNotifier`, `PushAlerts::queue()` (static list flushed in `app()->terminating`; tests swap `PushNotifier` for a recording fake), `PushController`, `resources/js/push.js`, `push`/`notificationclick` in `sw.js`.
- **Surveys:** `Survey`/`SurveyQuestion`/`SurveyResponse` (`Survey::liveInWard()`, `SurveyQuestion::choices()`), `SurveyRecorder` (every channel; phone only as a hash, unique per survey; SMS/USSD may be partial), `SurveyResults` (summary, breakdowns, quotas, `intentionShares()` for the zone engine), `Console\SurveyController` (builder: questions posted as JSON), field runner (`surveyRunner` in `resources/js/field-forms.js`, sync type `survey_response` → `AnswerSurvey`), `PublicSurveyController` (`/s/{token}`), `SurveyPollController` (`/api/poll/ussd|sms/{token}`, CSRF-exempt, token from `App\Support\SurveyPoll`; USSD replays the input history, only END writes, screens ≤ 182 chars). Store plain data in IndexedDB (`JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(...))`), never Alpine proxies.
- **AI messaging and media:** Claude through the official PHP SDK only in `App\Services\Ai\AnthropicModel` (bound to `LanguageModel`; tests swap a fake); every call goes through `Ai\Claude::ask()` (monthly budget, `ai_calls` log with tokens and cost). `Ai\Prompts::system()` is the stable cached prefix (instructions + active `PolicyDocument`s; no dates or volatile data); per-request text after it, with free text passed through `Ai\Scrub`. **Never put names, phone numbers or issue descriptions in prompts**: segment labels and counts only. Long AI work runs as queued jobs (`DraftMessage`, `SuggestNarrativeGroups`) and pages poll with `waitFor(url)`. `MessageController` + `PolicyController`; `Broadcasting\{Audience, BroadcastDispatcher, SmsSender, SmsText}` + `SendBroadcastBatch` (recipients are voter/user ids; numbers read only when sending); `SmsCallbackController` (delivery, opt-out, inbox STOP → `SmsOptOut`, keyed hashes); `NarrativeController` (+ field sync type `narrative_report` → `ReportNarrative`), `Narratives` (trends, spike alerts), `ComplaintsController`, `NewsTracker` + `NewsController`, `PagePostController`; API keys via `App\Support\Secrets` (encrypted settings, `.env` wins). Config in `config/messaging.php`.
- **Performance and privacy (phase 8):** `App\Support\Aggregates::remember($name, $viewer, $params, fn)` caches heavy figures for `AGGREGATE_CACHE_SECONDS` (300; 0 in tests) per viewer area, storing plain data only (models as id references; never enable `cache.serializable_classes`); call `Aggregates::flush()` when something changes how figures are computed; `BackgroundRunner::warm()` pre-computes the statewide dashboard. Count per day in SQL with `Time::sqlLocalDate($column)`, never by loading rows. `App\Services\Erasure` (erase a voter or a number: personal fields cleared, anonymous fields and counts kept, number opted out; `applyRetention()` in batches after `Erasure::retentionDate()`), `DataRequest` + `PrivacyController` (public form, checked by phone) + `Console\DataRequestController`, SMS "DELETE" in `SmsCallbackController`; `App\Services\Backup` (AES-256 zip, no passwords/tokens/settings). Aggregates keep counting erased voters; lists of people exclude them.
- **Volunteers and demo data:** `VolunteerIntake` (shared by `JoinController`'s `/join` page and `POST /api/volunteers/{token}`), `Console\VolunteerController` (inbox, "Make agent" → `TeamController::share()` invite). `App\Services\DemoData` loads/removes fictional data; every row goes in `demo_records` so removal is exact; add new tables to its `TABLES` order (children first). Never invent past election results in demo data.
- **Operator tasks:** `Console\SystemController` (Update database, register import/confirm, pinger), `Console\SettingsController` + `App\Support\SettingsRegistry` (every owner decision with a placeholder default; read with `Settings::get/int`), `Console\UserController` + `App\Services\UserDirectory`, `Console\AuditController`.
- **Background work:** `App\Support\BackgroundRunner` (after-response runs, `/cron/{token}`, `app:tick`). Add periodic tasks to `BackgroundRunner::tasks()` with `everyMinutes()/dailyAt()/weekly()` slots, not to `routes/console.php`.
- **PWA:** `public/sw.js` (VERSION bumped by the build script; precaches `/build/manifest.json`; caches only the `DATA_PAGES` allowlist), manifest served by `PwaController`, icons from `scripts/make-icons.mjs`.
- **Deploy:** `scripts/build-shared-hosting.sh`, `deploy/shared-hosting/` (`index.php` finds `command-center/` next to `public_html` and fills `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` secrets on first request), `docs/DEPLOY-SHARED-HOSTING.md`.

## Rules

- Never push with failing tests or `pint --test` failures. Screenshot new screens at 360/768/1280, light and dark, and fix overflow before a phase is done.
- The repo is public: no real personal data, API keys or the owner's email address, in code, fixtures or commits (tests use `example.com` and fake `0800` numbers; `GuardTest` checks).
- Data protection (brief §9): no religion, ethnicity or PVC/VIN per person; age band, not date of birth; voter phones encrypted with a keyed hash (`App\Support\Phone::hash`) for de-duplication; exports admin-only and audit-logged with row counts (`Audit::record(..., rows: n)`); pages with phone numbers are never cached by the service worker.
- Migrations: name long indexes explicitly (MySQL caps identifiers at 64 characters; `GuardTest` checks), and remember a failed MySQL migration leaves its tables behind.
- Blade: never a directive straight after a letter or digit (`KB@if`), never a one-line `@php(...)` before a `@php … @endphp` block, never `</x-slot>@if` on one line (all guarded by `GuardTest`).
- JS: read a form's URL with `form.getAttribute('action')`.
- Accessibility: axe-core finds no WCAG A/AA violations today; keep it that way (text on colour needs 4.5:1, give progress bars and icon links names, never fade content to show state).
- Mobile: grid children get `min-width: 0` (in base CSS); wide tables sit in `x-table` (scrolls inside the card) and hide secondary columns on phones; 48px targets in the field app; inputs are 16px on phones.
- Deferred alerts: a static pending list flushed in `app()->terminating`, not `dispatch()->afterResponse()`. Ignore far-future timestamps when deciding what's "new". `refresh()` a model before finishing a background job. Phone search strips a leading `234`/`0` (`Phone::searchDigits`).
- Times are stored in UTC and shown in Africa/Lagos (`App\Support\Time`).
